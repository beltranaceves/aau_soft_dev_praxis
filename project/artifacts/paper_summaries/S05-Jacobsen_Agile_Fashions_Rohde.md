# S05: Investigating Agile Management Fashions at Rohde & Schwarz (Model Exam Paper)

## Source
Jacobsen, R. M. (2019). Investigating Agile Management Fashions at Rohde & Schwartz: Mindfully Navigating the Landscape of Agile Development Methods. Aalborg University.

## What This Is
A **student exam paper** — applies Cram & Newell's (2016) management fashion theory to analyze agile adoption at Rohde & Schwarz Technology Center (RnD team of 14 people).

## Problem Statement
*How does a company with an eclectic development methodology navigate and adopt agile development?*

## Methodology
- **Single case study**: Rohde & Schwarz Technology Center in Aalborg
- **Data**: 4 semi-structured interviews (UX & development, Product Manager, QA, Team Lead)
- **Analysis**: Thematic coding based on the 5 management fashion indicators

## Key Findings

### Norm of Progress (2 instances)
- Team is open to novel techniques
- PM: "It's important to combine someone that knows how to do it and the people with domain knowledge"
- TL: "Today you will giggle because of it (waterfall)... It may happen to SCRUM one day"

### Norm of Rationality (3 instances)
- New hires review methods critically ("So, now that you have been here for a while, what do you think?")
- Team collectively agrees on what works — shared understanding
- Open to trying new methods if recommended

### Socio-psychological Forces (4 instances)
- Methods must allow developers to be creative and experiment
- **Designated process days** where team democratically decides what works and what doesn't
- Methods serve the team's needs, not the other way around

### Techno-economic Forces (14 instances — most frequent)
- STRONG contrast between agile development team and waterfall mother company
- QA: "From the outside, we may seem waterfall-driven... R&S is a conventional firm that thinks in waterfall. We try to internally navigate in an agile manner."
- Agile practices support collaborative culture — Friday meetings for sharing

### Innovation Devolution (6 instances)
- Team employs specific techniques from agile BUT with specific goals in mind
- TL: "You always need to remember that a process is fantastic in its prescription, but it is not creating any value in itself"
- Tailoring is done reflectively and mindfully

## Discussion with Mintzberg
- R&S Technology Centre exhibits **Innovative organization** (adhocracy) with mutual adjustment
- Selected decentralization enables innovation
- Suggestion: More collaboration with business units, customers, and stakeholders could increase innovation further

## Overall Classification: **Tailor** (mindful)
- Five years of modifying, replacing, and adopting various agile methods
- Highly reflective practice
- Pragmatic — methods serve the team, not vice versa
- Mindful tailoring, not mindless dabbling

## How to Use This in Interview Analysis
1. **Compare**: How does Norlys's agile adoption compare to R&S? Is it mindful tailoring or something else?
2. **Look for fashion indicators** in interview transcripts
3. **Analyze the contrast** between what's said externally (waterfall) vs. what's done internally (agile)
4. **Use Mintzberg to interpret**: Is Norlys an innovative organization (adhocracy) or something else?
5. **Assess methodological reflection**: Do Norlys practitioners critically evaluate their methods like R&S does?

# S02: Discussion Questions for Lyytinen, Rose & Yoo (2010)

## Source
Discussion questions prepared for Lyytinen, Rose, & Yoo (2010). "Learning routines and disruptive technological change: Hyper-learning in seven software development organizations during internet adoption." *Information Technology & People*, 23(2), 165-192.

## Purpose
These questions are designed to guide understanding and discussion of the hyper-learning theory.

## Key Questions for Understanding the Theory

### Research Design Questions
- **Multi-site, theory-building case study**: How are cases selected? What data is collected? How is data collected and analyzed?
- The original study selected 7 SDOs (from 10 candidates) that were early, fast, continuous adopters of web technologies during the dot-com boom (1995-2000)

### Core Conceptualizations (to understand and exemplify)
1. **Distributed gate-keeping**: Knowledge acquisition is distributed across frontline developers rather than centralized in R&D
2. **Brokering external knowledge**: Acquiring end-products of external knowledge (components, outsourcing) rather than building internally
3. **Use of simple design rules and artifacts**: Using flexible, simple patterns and guidelines instead of rigid methodologies
4. **Building peer networks**: Mentoring, Q&A, peer-to-peer knowledge sharing

### Parallel Ambidexterity
- Exploration and exploitation happening simultaneously in the same unit
- Fast switching between the two modes
- Differs from sequential ambidexterity (alternating over time) and structural ambidexterity (separate units)

### Application Questions
- What contemporary technologies demand hyper-learning today? (e.g., generative AI, cloud computing)
- What would be the requirements for a system supporting hyper-learning?

## How to Use This in Interview Analysis
- Use these questions as a lens to examine how Norlys handles learning and knowledge
- Look for evidence of the four learning routines in interview transcripts
- Assess whether Norlys exhibits parallel ambidexterity or more traditional learning forms
- Consider what "disruptive technologies" Norlys is currently facing

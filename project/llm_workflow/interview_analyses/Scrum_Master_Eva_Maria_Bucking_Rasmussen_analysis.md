## Phase 5: Synthesis

### 5.1 Fieldstone Collection

**Theme: The real Scrum Master work**
> *"My main role is to remove obstacles for the developers and they don't always say, oh, this is an issue. Help me with this. So sometimes I just have to listen in and just if I hear something where they're complaining that this shit doesn't work, then I can jump in."* (Q11)

**Theme: Business not on board with agile**
> *"I would like for the business to work in a more Agile way. I think Agile is mostly represented in IT... if we in IT say we're working in iterations and we're planning and everything's good and the business is expecting us to do things right now and we'll be like we can't do that because we plan differently that's difficult."* (Q21)

**Theme: Plans are provisional**
> *"These plans are not set in stone. They can change and they can change tomorrow, even though we yesterday talked about how it's supposed to be."* (Q24)

**Theme: Developer happiness as metric**
> *"I think if it's a good sprint for me, I think it's a sprint where the developers are content and have had a good sprint, so if they have good days, it makes me happy."* (Q33)

**Theme: Non-IT background as guardrail**
> *"I think it's also a strength because I get to ask the stupid questions... If they can't explain what they're going to do in a way that I can understand it, then it's not described well enough."* (Q46)

**Theme: Ceremonial vs. actual practice**
> *"We rarely finish [sprint tasks]... we probably work more in a Kanban kind of way."* (Q33 combined)

**Theme: "Too nice" to say no**
> *"I think we're just too nice, so we don't do that. We just say, yeah, we'll do it right away."* (Q42)

**Theme: People over process**
> *"My role is to make sure that they have the best options and the best workday so they have fun coding."* (Q44)
